Calculation Methodology & References

Primary reference: Youd, T.L. and Idriss, I.M. (2001). "Liquefaction Resistance of Soils: Summary Report from the 1996 NCEER and 1998 NCEER/NSF Workshops on Evaluation of Liquefaction Resistance of Soils." Journal of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering, ASCE, 127(4), 297–313.

QuantityEquation usedEq. #
This is the default (only) methodology implemented: the NCEER/NSF workshop "simplified procedure." No other liquefaction method (Boulanger & Idriss, Cetin, Moss, Andrus & Stokoe) is used or blended in. Valid only for level to gently sloping ground, Holocene alluvial/fluvial soils, depths < ~15–20 m, non-gravelly soils.
Calculation engine is separate from the UI (see Engine object in source). All intermediate values are traceable via "Show calculation" in the results table. Built for engineering review — verify independently before use on real projects.
